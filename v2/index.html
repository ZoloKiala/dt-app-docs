    Limpopo Digital Twin Portal¶

    Documentation for the TerriaJS portal showcasing the Digital Twin (DT) of the Limpopo River Basin, developed for the International Water Management Institute (IWMI).

    About this documentation

    This is the v2 guide — it documents the portal now running at limpopo.digitaltwins.iwmi.org (TerriaMap 0.4.1 / TerriaJS 8.9.5, built 2026-05-15). The screenshots are captured from that release. For the original 2024 portal, choose v1 in the version selector in the header.

    Open the portal

    Launch the live portal at limpopo.digitaltwins.iwmi.org. See Release Notes for what changed since the 2024 release — a redesigned interface, five new catalogue categories, and new tools including an AI chatbot and a SWAT extraction tool.

    What the portal does¶

    The WebGIS Portal (Phase 2) is an advanced geospatial web portal that visualises and lets you interact with spatial datasets relevant to the Limpopo River basin. It features a Digital Twin of the basin, aggregating and interpolating data from satellite imagery, in-situ sensors and numerical model outputs.

    Phase 2 builds on the foundation established in Phase 1, using the TerriaJS framework — an open-source platform for web-based geospatial catalogue exploration. The portal draws inspiration from platforms such as Digital Earth Africa (DE Africa) and NationalMap.

    Key capabilities at a glance¶

    Capability What it gives you
    Geospatial visualisation Interactive 2D and 3D views of basin data
    Thematic data catalogue Curated datasets across eleven thematic categories
    Time series analysis Explore change over time with a timeline slider
    Workbench Toggle, restyle, reorder and compare active layers
    My Data Add local files or web services without uploading them
    Stories Capture scenes into a narrative you can share
    Share & print Shareable URLs, PNG export and printable views
    AI chatbot (Water Copilot) Ask questions about the basin and get guided answers
    SWAT Extraction Tool Query modelled flows for a channel over a date range

    New in v2

    Compared with the 2024 release, v2 adds five catalogue categories (Flood Monitoring & Early Warning, Drought Monitoring & Risk Assessment, Citizen Science, Water Use Monitoring and Analysis, and Data Sources & Providers), two new tools (the Water Copilot chatbot and the SWAT Extraction Tool), an expanded basemap set, and a redesigned workbench. Signing in is now required to open the portal.

    Conclusion of the source report¶

    The WebGIS Portal represents a significant advancement in geospatial data visualisation for IWMI. Built on the robust TerriaJS framework, it integrates diverse datasets and provides an intuitive interface for exploring the Limpopo River basin — supporting informed decision-making in water resource management across the basin.

    Contact¶

    For help with the portal, or for questions about the datasets it publishes, contact the IWMI geospatial team at iwmigeospatial@gmail.com, or use the Feedback form built into the portal.
