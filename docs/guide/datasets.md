# Dataset Themes

The [Data Catalogue](data-catalogue.md) groups data into **eleven** thematic
categories in v2. This page describes what each one holds. The mechanics of
previewing, adding, styling and charting any dataset are the same throughout —
see [Data Catalogue](data-catalogue.md).

  <figcaption>Figure 4: The eleven thematic categories in the v2 Data Catalogue.</figcaption>
</figure>

## Natural Basin Characteristic

The physical setting of the basin. Sub-collections include:

- **Physiography** — elevation and topography, basin and sub-basin boundaries,
  the channel network.
- **3D point clouds** — high-resolution drone-surveyed elevation for the
  Oliphants River and the Crocodile River System (Upper and Lower).
- **Land cover and land use** — Land Use, Irrigated Mapping and the Vegetation
  Condition Index (VCI); several are available as time series.
- **Climate** — CHIRPS historical rainfall, rainfall monitoring and forecast,
  and rainfall anomaly charts.

## Flood Monitoring & Early Warning

!!! note "New in v2"
    Layers for monitoring flooding across the basin and supporting early-warning —
    observed and forecast flood extent and related indicators.

## Drought Monitoring & Risk Assessment

!!! note "New in v2"
    Drought monitoring and risk layers, complementing the climate and vegetation
    datasets for assessing drought onset, severity and exposure.

## Socio-Economic Profile

Socio-economic conditions within the basin, including population and
demographic factors. Population data can be styled from the **display**
dropdown — by sub-basin, area, polygon ID or population.

## Water Resources

The availability, distribution and use of water resources:

- **Surface Water Sources** — including discharge monitoring from the SWAT
  model (present-day, gauging stations and forecast).
- **Infrastructure** — including dam locations.

## Citizen Science

!!! note "New in v2"
    Community-contributed observations of river and ecosystem health — for example
    **miniSASS** (river biomonitoring) and **FishTRAC**. These bring ground
    observations alongside the modelled and remotely-sensed layers.

## Water Use Monitoring and Analysis

!!! note "New in v2"
    Water accounting and water-use monitoring layers — how much water is available
    and how it is being used across the basin.

## Ecosystems

Datasets on the basin's ecosystems and environmental flows:

| Dataset | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **E-flow Regions** | Ecological flow regions |
| **E-flow Monitoring and Forecasting** | Current and forecast ecological flows |
| **E-flow Alert Levels** | Levels of ecological flow alert |
| **SWAT Incremental Contributions** | Incremental contributions from the SWAT model |
| **E-flow Warnings** | Alerts on ecological flow conditions |

E-flow charts expose attributes such as **Availability Surplus**, **Deficit**,
**SWAT Present Flow** and **E-Flow**; hover a point on the curve to read its
values.

## Satellite Images

Imagery for remote sensing and environmental monitoring, including **Rolling
Monthly GeoMAD (Sentinel-2)** and the **Water Observations from Space**
all-time summary.

## Data Sources & Providers

!!! note "New in v2"
    Provenance for the catalogue — who supplies each dataset and where it comes
    from — gathered into one category so you can trace a layer back to its source.
