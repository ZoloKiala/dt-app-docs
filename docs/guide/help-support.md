## The Help feature

The **Help** button in the top bar gathers the portal's guidance in one place —
the tour, how-to videos and step-by-step guides. On first load a coach-mark
points at it: *"Find the Tour, how-to videos & other help content here."*

<figure markdown="span">
  ![The Help feature](../assets/v2/v2-help.png)
  <figcaption>Figure 15: The Help feature.</figcaption>
</figure>

It includes:

- **Take the Tour** — a guided walkthrough that names each control; click
  **Start Tour** to begin.
- **Getting Started with the Map** — a video and instructions on the basics:
  searching, exploring datasets, interacting with layers, and changing the
  basemap.
- **Step-by-Step Guides** — detailed instructions for common workflows.
- **Share & Story Migration** — how to update share links from older versions
  of TerriaJS.
- **Guides and contact** — reach the support team at
  [iwmigeospatial@gmail.com](mailto:iwmigeospatial@gmail.com).

## The About page

The **About** button (top bar) opens information about the Limpopo Digital Twin
concept — the platform's purpose, its open-data architecture, and guidance on
using the portal.

## Feedback

The **Give Feedback** tool sends your comments or questions directly to the
developers.

<figure markdown="span">
  ![The feedback form](../assets/v2/v2-feedback.png)
  <figcaption>Figure 16: The feedback form.</figcaption>
</figure>

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Your name** | Optional |
| **Email address** | Optional, but required if you want a follow-up |
| **Comment or question** | Your feedback or enquiry |
| **Share my map view** | Sends your current map view for context |
| **Send** | Submits the feedback |

## Account

Because v2 requires sign-in, the top bar also carries **Account** (manage your
profile) and **Log out**. See [Getting Started](getting-started.md#signing-in).
