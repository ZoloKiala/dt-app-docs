    Dataset Themes¶

    The catalogue groups data into five thematic categories. This page walks through each one and what you will find inside. The mechanics of previewing, adding and styling any dataset are the same throughout — see Data Catalogue.

    Natural Basin Characteristics¶

    Opening this category's dropdown reveals its sub-collections.

    Physiography¶

    Lists elevation and topography, basin and sub-basin boundaries, the channel network, and 3D point cloud data.

    Figure 19: Exploring the Natural Basin Characteristics datasets.

    Three controls are worth knowing on this view:

    • Dropdown menu — display dataset features such as Ideal Zoom, data details and display variables.
    • Three-dot icon — edit the dataset's style, export the map, or remove the dataset from the map view and workbench.
    • Vertical icons — positioned along the right of the map; hover over any of them for a description of what it does.

    The display variable control also opens up charting and filtering for the data.

    3D point clouds¶

    High-resolution 3D point cloud data for river systems in South Africa, currently covering the Oliphants River and the Crocodile River System (Upper and Lower). The data was collected by drone survey.

    Click a dataset to preview it and add it to the map. Beneath the preview sits the detailed description and the rest of the dataset's metadata.

    Land cover and land use¶

    Contains the Land Use, Irrigated Mapping and Vegetation Condition Index (VCI) datasets.

    Some of these datasets offer advanced features:

    • Time series — for datasets such as irrigated mapping, use the time series controls beneath the map view and in the workbench panel. The left and right arrows step through time.
    • Map styles — change and customise map styles as needed for flexible visualisation.

    Climate¶

    Contains CHIRPS Historical Rainfall, Rainfall Monitoring and Forecast, and Rainfall Anomaly Circle Charts.

    Click on the data for more information about each feature, including the raw data and an advanced chart.

    Socio-Economic Profile¶

    Data on socio-economic conditions within the basin, including population and other demographic factors.

    Click Data Preview to preview the map.

    Figure 35: Population data on the map view, drawn by the selected display variable.

    Figure 36: Population data on the map view for a different display variable.

    The demographic dataset can be styled from the display dropdown: disable styling altogether, or style by sub-basin, area, polygon ID or population.

    Water Resources¶

    Datasets covering the availability, distribution and usage of water resources in the region:

    • Surface Water Sources
    • Infrastructure

    Other datasets in this category are explored the same way.

    Ecosystems¶

    Datasets related to the ecosystems of the Limpopo River basin:

    Dataset Contents
    E-flow Regions Various ecological flow regions
    E-flow Monitoring and Forecasting Current and forecast ecological flows
    E-flow Alert Levels Different levels of ecological flow alert
    SWAT Incremental Contributions Incremental contributions from the SWAT model
    E-flow Warnings Alerts related to ecological flow conditions

    Satellite Images¶

    Satellite imagery for remote sensing and environmental monitoring, including Rolling Monthly GeoMAD (Sentinel-2) and the Water Observations from Space all-time summary.
